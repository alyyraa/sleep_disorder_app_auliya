About the Application

XGBoost implementation for web-based sleep-disorder and stress-level prediction.

Background

Sleep disorders and stress levels can affect physical health, mental wellbeing, and daily productivity. This academic application uses XGBoost to provide an initial prediction based on lifestyle and health information.

XGBoost Models

XGBoost Classifier predicts Sleep Disorder as None, Insomnia, or Sleep Apnea.

XGBoost Regressor predicts Stress Level on a 1–10 scale. The models are independent, so their results should be interpreted separately.

Dataset and System

The Training Dataset is based on the Sleep Health and Lifestyle Dataset. Patient records are managed separately and are used only for prediction.

The system includes authentication, master data, model training, evaluation, prediction history, dashboard analytics, and PDF/Excel reports.
